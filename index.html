Rebuilding respect

  • Keep what people tell you private, every time.
  • Speak well of people who are not in the room.
  • Share less, and let people see the change over months.
  • If you hurt one person badly, give one short, sincere apology.
  • Stay warm, but choose carefully who gets your trust.
